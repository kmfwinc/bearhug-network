When browsing turns into a full search process

While 1-off intros may get the job done, often times a full search process is required. Every Bearhug search engagement includes a complimentary Structural Growth Review where we'll map your leadership team against the leadership benchmark we've developed over 30 years of category-specific searches. You'll walk away with a concrete 90-day Topgrading and sequencing recommendation whether you engage us for a formal search or not. When you do, every search includes a comp benchmarking deck for the role and a qualified shortlist delivered in 21 days.

If we can't present a candidate worth interviewing in 30 days, we will cut you a check for $10,000 and keep working until we do.
